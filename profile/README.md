## 📱 Flagship Spotlight: Shondhaan (সবকিছুর সন্ধান, এক প্ল্যাটফর্মে)

**[Shondhaan](https://shondhaan.com)** is Bangladesh's premier on-demand hyperlocal Super-App, engineered to solve everyday service, commerce, and career needs within a single, unified mobile experience.

### 🧩 Super-App Service Matrix
* 🔧 **Home Services (সার্ভিস):** On-demand verified technicians for AC repair/wash, plumbing, electrical maintenance, deep cleaning, pest control, and shifting.
* 🛒 **Mart (মার্ট):** Express multi-vendor grocery & household essentials store with dynamic cart, scheduled delivery, and coupon engine.
* 🤝 **Deals (ডিল):** Zero-middleman classifieds marketplace with instant in-app buyer/seller chat and localized ad listings.
* 💼 **Jobs (চাকরি):** Career hub offering 1-tap digital CV submissions, video resume studio, and live application progress tracking.
* 🎓 **Exam (পরীক্ষা ও স্কিল):** Standardized technical MCQ assessments with verified skill badges embedded into digital resumes.
* 🚚 **Logistics & Hub (আরও সেবা):** On-demand parcel courier dispatch, city ride-sharing, food delivery, and emergency hotline directory.

### 🏗️ Mobile Engineering Architecture
The Shondhaan mobile application represents modern Flutter engineering at enterprise scale:
* **UI/UX Paradigm:** Pathao-inspired Super-App UI with floating glass capsules (`BackdropFilter` sigma-12 blur), dual-layer ambient elevation, and 100% interactive affordances.
* **Bengali-First Localization:** Deep typography integration using **Hind Siliguri**, localized Bengali numerals (`৳ ১,২৫০`), and bilingual toggle support.
* **Modern State & Navigation:** Powered by **Riverpod 2.6** for reactive state isolation and **GoRouter 14.8** for declarative deep-linking and subscreen routing.
* **Microservice Backend Mesh:** High-performance, decoupled microservices for Identity, Services (`backend-yservice`), Mart (`backend-ymart`), Deals (`backend-deal`), and Career (`backend-yjob`).
